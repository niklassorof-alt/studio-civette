Studio Civette is a Berlin food studio specialised in creating memorable events: brand launches, PR events and set catering. It was founded by a chef trained in Cape Town and Berlin’s fine dining kitchens, including Ernst, a Michelin-starred restaurant. We work independently of any restaurant, so every menu starts from your brief and in our style. Registered in Berlin and fully food-safety certified.
