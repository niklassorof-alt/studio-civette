Studio Civette is a Berlin-based food studio for brands who understand that how something is served says as much as what it says. We build bespoke tasting experiences for launches and activations — each menu composed with the same care, restraint, and intent a brand brings to its own identity.

The studio

Founded by a chef trained in Berlin's fine dining scene, including time at Ernst (Michelin-starred), Studio Civette operates independently — without the structure of a traditional restaurant.

Fully food-safety certified, registered, and based in Berlin.
